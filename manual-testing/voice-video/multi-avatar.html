The show runs itself: the page connects all three, the host opens with a scene suggestion and names who starts, the actors alternate for Actor turns per scene turns (3 by default), then the host calls "Scene!" and opens the next one, and so on until Stop. Avatars cannot hear each other (each has its own downlink), so the finished text of each turn is passed to the next avatar as a typed turn. Only the avatar holding the floor may speak: a line nobody asked for (the service checks in on its own after about two minutes of no input) is interrupted at once and never routed. Your mic (Talk) or a typed line on any card joins the scene from that avatar. Mute / volume / speaker must land only on that card's element(s).
